Malls in Pasig

Estancia Mall
★ ★ ★ ★ ★

A premier lifestyle destination in Pasig, Estancia Mall offers a mix of high-end retail brands, gourmet dining options, and entertainment facilities. It features a modern design with open spaces, making it a favorite spot for shopping and leisure.

SM East Ortigas
★ ★ ★ ★ ★

Conveniently located along Ortigas Avenue, SM East Ortigas is a one-stop shop for everyday needs. It houses a wide range of stores, from fashion and accessories to electronics and home goods, plus a supermarket and food court for quick and easy meals.

Robinsons Galleria
★ ★ ★ ★ ★

One of the most iconic malls in the area, Robinsons Galleria boasts over 400 stores, including international brands, local boutiques, and a variety of dining choices. It also has a cinema complex and event spaces for gatherings and shows.
