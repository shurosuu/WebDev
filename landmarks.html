Landmarks of Pasig

Places that define the city's character and history

Pasig Cathedral

The Pasig Cathedral, formally known as the Parish of Saint Pius X, is one of the most recognizable religious landmarks in the city. With its striking modern architecture and soaring facade, it stands apart from the traditional Spanish-era churches found elsewhere in the Philippines. The cathedral serves as a spiritual home for thousands of Pasig residents and is a venue for significant religious celebrations throughout the year, particularly during Christmas and Holy Week.

Rainforest Adventure Experience (RAVE) Park

The Rainforest Adventure Experience, popularly known as RAVE Park, is one of Pasig's most exciting outdoor destinations. Nestled within a lush, forested environment, it offers a wide range of thrilling activities including zip lines, obstacle courses, wall climbing, and nature trails that cater to both kids and adults. It is the perfect escape from the urban jungle, giving visitors a taste of adventure and nature without leaving the city. RAVE Park is a favorite among families, school groups, and thrill-seekers looking for a fun and memorable day out in Pasig.

Capitol Commons

Capitol Commons is one of Pasig's most vibrant mixed-use developments, built on the former grounds of the Rizal Provincial Capitol. It seamlessly blends open green spaces with upscale residential towers, restaurants, retail shops, and entertainment venues. The centerpiece is a beautifully landscaped park where families and friends gather to relax, jog, or simply enjoy the outdoors. It has quickly become one of the most popular lifestyle destinations in the east side of Metro Manila.

Plaza Bonifacio

Plaza Bonifacio is a historic public plaza located in the heart of old Pasig, named in honor of Andres Bonifacio, the great plebeian hero of the Philippine Revolution. The plaza serves as a gathering place for the community during civic events, fiestas, and local celebrations. Surrounded by heritage structures and the old town center, it stands as a quiet reminder of Pasig's rich colonial and revolutionary past, offering visitors a glimpse into the city's deep-rooted history.
